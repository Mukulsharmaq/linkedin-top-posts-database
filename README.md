## Open it
- **[posts.csv](posts.csv)**: every post on one row, with a direct link, likes, comments, shares, format and length. GitHub shows it as a searchable table, so type a name to filter.
- **[creators.csv](creators.csv)**: the 86 accounts, with each one's number of 100+ posts, median likes and comments, and best post.

## Ask Claude
1. Download [posts.csv](https://github.com/Mukulsharmaq/linkedin-top-posts-database/raw/main/posts.csv) (and [creators.csv](https://github.com/Mukulsharmaq/linkedin-top-posts-database/raw/main/creators.csv) if you want the account view).
2. Open [claude.ai](https://claude.ai), attach the file and ask in plain words.

Try these
- Which 10 creators get the most comments per post, and which format do they use most?
- In the last 3 months, do image posts beat text-only posts on comments?
- Show me the 20 best posts under 100 words, with their links.

## How it was built
1. Picked the agencies and coaches who write LinkedIn content for B2B founders and execs, plus the creators that the agencies and their clients follow.
2. Found who they write for from case studies, testimonials, client logos and public engagement.
3. Pulled each account's recent public posts with an Apify LinkedIn post scraper (no login).
4. Loaded everything into one SQLite database with engagement snapshots, then kept the posts with 100+ likes.

**Next stage:** breaking each post into a reusable blueprint: the words that carry the pattern, the parts you swap for your own story, and the parts you drop.

## Columns

| posts.csv | engagement_score | likes + 2 × comments + 4 × shares |
| posts.csv | post_url | the original post |
| creators.csv | posts_100_plus | posts from this account in posts.csv |
| creators.csv | median_likes, median_comments | across those posts |
| creators.csv | top_post_likes, top_post_url | the account's best post here |

## Notes
- Post text isn't included. Every row links to the original post on LinkedIn.
- Counts are a snapshot from the date in `counts_as_of`.
- Accounts were pulled to different depths (most the last 1 to 6 months, a few up to 2 years), so compare medians, not post counts.
- Want an account added or removed? Open an issue.
